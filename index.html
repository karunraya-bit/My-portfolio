Inventory Management

Built a comprehensive system to to systematically track organizational assets and streamline resource allocation. The platform monitors the entire lifecycle of physical equipment and consumables through a digital check-in/check-out registry that establishes clear accountability. By integrating automated threshold notifications, the system proactively alerts administrators to low stock levels to prevent supply chain disruptions. The solution minimizes manual audit discrepancies, reduces asset loss, and generates analytical reports on resource utilization.
